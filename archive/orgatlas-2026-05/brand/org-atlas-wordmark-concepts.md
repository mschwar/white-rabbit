# Org Atlas Wordmark Concepts

These five directions are grounded in the product cues present in the local Org Atlas materials: lead intelligence, wide-net discovery, transparent validation, confidence ranking, exports, and customer-specific guardrails.

## 01 / Wide Net

`Org Atlas` with the `O` turned into a minimal evidence net.

Why it fits: the product should cast a wide net, then connect raw candidates into explainable signal.

## 02 / Evidence Joins

`Org |check| Atlas` using a restrained operator between the words.

Why it fits: the brand promise is not just finding names; it is cross-checking and showing why the result can be trusted.

## 03 / Query Route

`org/atlas` in lowercase, with the slash treated as a route from request to result.

Why it fits: the interface is meant to feel like a simple natural-language input that turns intent into usable output.

## 04 / Ranked Signal

`ORG ATLAS` with a small sorted-list motif feeding the wordmark.

Why it fits: the product needs to return many hits while making quality legible through ranking and confidence.

## 05 / Guarded Atlas

`Org Atlas` inside minimal bracket corners.

Why it fits: the product is intentionally bounded by demo caps, lead-generation scope, and customer data isolation.

## Recommendation

The strongest starting points are:

- `01 / Wide Net` if the logo should carry the product idea quickly.
- `02 / Evidence Joins` if the brand should feel more operational and B2B.
- `05 / Guarded Atlas` if the first use is a controlled demo or sales deck.
